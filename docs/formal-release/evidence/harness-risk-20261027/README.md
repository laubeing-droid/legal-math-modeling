On one ambient probability space (Omega, m0, mu), each before-attempt information field past(t) is a sub-sigma-algebra. The bad event is measurable in the ambient space; it need not already be measurable in the past. Its conditional probability is the conditional expectation of its indicator. Summing the resulting marginal bounds gives the finite union bound without independence. The stronger random-budget statement bounds cumulative failure by the expected sum of integrable history-dependent budgets. A filtration is covered, but monotonicity of the information fields is not needed for this implication.

For countably many attempts, nonnegative summable deterministic budgets imply that the probability of any failure is at most their total sum. The proof uses actual countable subadditivity of mu and ENNReal/real conversion with finite-measure and summability conditions. No finite-horizon test or finite list is promoted to a countable proof.
