## Withdrawal rule

For a fixed finite horizon and fixed deterministic alpha schedule, withdraw only guarantees that depend on a failed premise. A changed target or legal branch keeps the result under the old target as history but does not transfer it to the new target. Failure of support/absolute continuity withdraws covariate-shift transport and derived target-risk conclusions. Failure of exchangeability withdraws the conformal result; failure of the IID/validation premises withdraws the finite selected-risk certificate that uses them. An unknown or too-low coverage lower bound means no ratio and no risk acceptance. If actual history-conditional validity or the fixed total budget is unknown or false, stop the continuing guarantee; do not extend it beyond the fixed horizon. Retain previously valid results under their original premises, and output that the new path is outside the current guarantee without inventing a replacement probability.
