# Actual fitting, sample calibration, and selective score consumption

Original topics 01 and 05 and the fourth edge in original `04_全阶段接口与连续消费.md` require the calibration consumer to read the actual fitted result. `runSamples` now calls `fitTable` on the supplied training observations, computes a calibration certificate from the supplied calibration observations and that exact fitted table, and returns the selected score and certificate. It does not merely compare two external fitting contracts or accept an externally supplied risk number.

Ten exact type and axiom targets passed [run 40407520488](https://github.com/laubeing-droid/legal-math-modeling/actions/runs/40407520488), [job 121869022779](https://github.com/laubeing-droid/legal-math-modeling/actions/runs/40407520488/job/121869022779), at subject `3ec7fe89cbafd09410f68400e923db3bc0dd208c`. `verification.json` binds both source files to that subject. The raw driver output preserves Lean indentation after removal of CI timestamps and ANSI escapes. Its ten unique targets use only standard axioms or no axioms.

Every successful pipeline result contains the actual empirical minimizer, its complete learning protocol, the fitted prediction table, selected cutoff, supplied radius and checked integer witness, sample size, candidate cutoffs, and computed coverage/risk bounds. The score consumer reads that fitted table at the actual query and applies the selected acceptance rule. Changing labels in the compiled fixtures changes the actual fit or refuses calibration, rather than merely changing a status annotation.

Grouping uses the real `EventId` on every observation. Repeated calibration event IDs and overlap between training and calibration events are refused. The legacy `FitContract.independentUnit` is a `MeasurementId`; it is not silently reinterpreted as an event. Event identity checks do not prove independence. The probability law is over visible feature/label pairs, while event IDs may be deterministic metadata.

The whole-pipeline theorem controls the entire predeclared finite model-by-cutoff family before either is selected. For family size `M`, it controls both statistics for all pairs with radius `sqrt(log(4*M/delta)/(2*n))`. On a measurable event of probability at least `1-delta`, every result actually returned by this pipeline has positive population accepted coverage and population selective loss-risk at most the target. The training history may share randomness with calibration; uniform control covers every member of the fixed family. Measurability, iid/common-law calibration observations, bounded population loss, a fixed nonempty family, positive sample size and `0<delta<1` remain explicit. Real data, source truth, legal authority and the sampling design are not established by passing synthetic examples.

The numerical radius is now checked by executable rational arithmetic. For a natural witness `k`, `radiusVerified` checks `4*M <= delta*2^k` and `k <= 2*n*radius^2`, together with positivity and nonnegative radius. Its theorem derives the logarithmic bound using `log(2^k) <= k`. The producer checks its cutoff family and the pipeline checks the full model-by-cutoff family. The whole-pipeline population theorem obtains its radius obligation from the actual accepted execution; it no longer assumes an externally asserted conservative radius. This sufficient certificate can conservatively refuse an input; it does not claim the smallest possible radius.
